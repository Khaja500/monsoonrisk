Weather derivatives

One traded contract and two constructed analogues. The index is an anchored cumulative deviation of rainfall from its 1991–2020 daily normal, so at the end of a season the settlement value is the realised seasonal rainfall in millimetres, and one millimetre is worth ₹50.

Part 2

Israeli (game) option on NIFTY 50

The holder may exercise at any time for G. The issuer may cancel at any time by paying H = G + δ. Neither side controls the other, so the value solves a Dynkin game, and on a tree the recursion is Vi = min( Hi , max( Gi , Ci ) ). The contract is hypothetical: nothing like it trades on NIFTY.

Limitations

  • Grid versus station. RAINMUMBAI settles on two IMD stations. The model is built on IMD's 0.25° grid, whose cell near Mumbai is about 23% wetter over the season than the station normal, so every series is rescaled onto the published settlement normal. That correction fixes the mean, not the shape.
  • The current season is provisional. IMD has not published the 2026 archive year. Days in 2026 come from NASA POWER, bias-corrected the same way, and are flagged as provisional everywhere. For Mumbai the provisional proxy overstates the official 2026 seasonal total, which is shown rather than hidden.
  • No market to calibrate against. India has no catastrophe bond market and no Israeli options. Bond prices are model-based and reported as sensitivities to the risk-premium rule; the game option is validated by limiting cases and cross-method agreement, not by fit to a market.
  • Forty-five seasons. Tail quantiles rest on a short record. Bootstrap intervals on the empirical quantiles are in the exported data for every index.
  • Independence across years. Multi-year bonds assume independent seasons. The lag-1 autocorrelation and the cross-city rank correlations are reported so the assumption can be checked.
  • Realised volatility is missing. Bulk NIFTY 50 history could not be downloaded, so India VIX is the volatility input and a volatility grid is shown instead of a realised-volatility estimate.
  • Constant volatility. The game option uses geometric Brownian motion, so no smile and no skew.
